## Mô tả
Five below, no power, in the middle of the Minnesota woods. A man outside begs to be let in. But the train didn't stop because of a fallen tree…
What would you do: open the door or keep it shut? Tell us in the comments! Part 2 is coming.

Animated POV short film (fiction). Inspired by the real problem of copper wire theft along rail lines. All characters, voices and sound effects were created for this video.
