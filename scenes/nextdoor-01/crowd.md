## Beat 6
@OKAFOR [groggy, annoyed]: What on earth was that?
@BRIANNA [scared]: Did something fall? My whole ceiling shook.
@DEB [worried]: Is everybody okay up here?
@TYLER [confused]: That was 4C. Nobody lives in 4C.
@MRS_LEE [frightened, quietly]: Not again. Please, not again.
@RAY [irritated]: Somebody call Pruitt.
@BRIANNA [nervous]: I did. He picked up on the first ring.
@OKAFOR [suspicious]: At midnight? He never picks up.
@DEB [anxious]: Do you smell that? Like burning plastic.
@TYLER [uneasy]: Since when is there a new lock on that door?
@MRS_LEE [whispers]: Don't look at it. Just go back inside.
@RAY [surprised]: Wait, my Wi-Fi just dropped.
@BRIANNA [scared]: Mine too. My phone says no network.
@OKAFOR [shaken]: My smoke detector just stopped blinking.
@DEB [confused]: So did mine. Isn't that weird?
@TYLER [nervous laugh]: Okay. This building is cursed.
@RAY [tense]: Should we knock? Somebody could be hurt in there.
@MRS_LEE [pleading, whispers]: No. Please. Nobody knocks.
@BRIANNA [worried]: Where's the new kid from 4B?
@DEB [quietly]: Right there. Look at him. He's white as a sheet.
